1. Stop Streamlit with Ctrl+C.
2. Extract the V3.3 hotfix.
3. Replace app.py, theme.py, ui_components.py and .streamlit/config.toml in your current project folder.
4. Do not replace venv, database or uploads.
5. Run run_app.bat.
6. Press Ctrl+Shift+R in the browser.
7. Toggle Light mode in the sidebar to verify the contrast change.
